# P43 Operations and Support Readiness

OperationsProductionReadiness: ReadyForApproval

Roles: ServiceOwnerRole, TechnicalOwnerRole, DeploymentOwnerRole, IncidentCommanderRole, RollbackAuthorityRole, SecurityEscalationRole, SupportOwnerRole, ChangeOwnerRole.

Support runbook: verify health, inspect container status, review redacted logs, check metrics, triage incidents, escalate by severity, rollback when criteria require it, capture evidence, close incident with follow-up.
